Deathmatch

Private 1v1, solo vs AI, or co-op vs bots — first to 10 takedowns. Pick your character, loadout, and arena — any of the 8 weapons, in black or white — and the game’s desktop, controller, or touch controls.

Arena

Difficulty changes bot speed, accuracy, and reaction time — Easy: slower, less accurate, slower to react; Normal: like a courtyard guard; Hard: faster, more accurate, quicker to react. Bots are computer opponents, always labeled BOT.

Modes: 1v1 Private match — first to 10 takedowns against one other player, via a room code. Solo vs AI — you vs 1–4 bots, first to 10 takedowns (your takedowns vs the bots’ takedowns); needs no relay and no code, and works even before the relay is configured. Co-op vs Bots — you and a friend as a team vs the bots, first team to 10; create a room and share the code, the room plays on the creator’s arena, and the joiner’s arena pick is ignored. Katana Duel — a melee duel with Rochelle’s dual katanas on the Theater stage, first to 3 hits; 1v1 is a private room over the relay, Solo is you vs one BOT. No loadout, no starting weapon, no arena pick — katanas only, Theater only. Bots are computer opponents, always labeled BOT — never real people. The relay pauses the match during a disconnect and allows 30 seconds to reconnect. Leaving longer forfeits the match. Loadout: pick a black or white finish and a starting weapon above — 13 weapons now: the 8 guns plus Knife (melee, silent, never runs out), Pen (2, thrown, sticks where it lands), Grenade (1, 2-second fuse, blast hurts anyone close — including you), Claymore (1, place it and it fires when an enemy crosses its front arc), and Sniper rifle (5, one-shot, scope on aim). In the match, Tab cycles weapons and 1–9, 0 select one directly (9 Knife, 0 Sniper rifle; throwing knife, grenade and claymore are Tab-only) on the courtyard. Ballroom needs a gun system before it can host a deathmatch. Deathmatch multiplayer needs the project’s Cloudflare Worker deployed and its URL set in assets/js/deathmatch-config.js — solo vs AI does not.
