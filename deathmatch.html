Courtyard Deathmatch

Private 1v1, first to 10 takedowns. Both players use the Courtyard, pistol and rifle loadout, and the game’s desktop, controller, or touch controls.

The relay pauses the match during a disconnect and allows 30 seconds to reconnect. Leaving longer forfeits the match. Deathmatch needs the project’s Cloudflare Worker deployed and its URL set in assets/js/deathmatch-config.js.
